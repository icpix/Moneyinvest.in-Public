# Smart Loan Calculator - Usage Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. **Basic Loan Calculation**
1. Enter your **Loan Amount** (minimum ₹1,000)
2. Set the **Annual Interest Rate** (e.g., 8.5%)
3. Choose **Tenure Type** (Years or Months)
4. Enter the **Loan Tenure** (e.g., 20 years)
5. Select your **Loan Start Date**
6. Click **"Calculate EMI"**

### 2. **Understanding the Results**
After calculation, you'll see:
- **Monthly EMI**: Your fixed monthly payment
- **Total Interest**: Interest you'll pay over the loan term
- **Total Amount**: Principal + Interest
- **Savings Section**: Shows benefits of prepayments (initially ₹0)

## 💡 Using Prepayment Features

### **How to Add Prepayments**
1. After calculating your EMI, scroll to the repayment schedule
2. In any month's "Prepayment" column, enter the amount you plan to prepay
3. The calculator automatically updates:
   - **Interest Saved**: Money saved due to prepayments
   - **Time Saved**: Months/years you'll finish the loan earlier
   - **Total Prepayment**: Sum of all prepayments

### **Prepayment Tips**
- ✅ **Early prepayments save more**: Prepaying in the first few years saves maximum interest
- ✅ **Any amount helps**: Even small prepayments make a difference
- ✅ **Regular prepayments**: Consider consistent monthly prepayments
- ✅ **Windfalls**: Use bonuses, tax refunds, or windfalls for prepayments

## 📊 Advanced Features

### **Summary Statistics**
The calculator shows additional insights:
- **Average Monthly Interest**: Helps understand interest burden
- **Average Monthly Principal**: Shows principal repayment pattern
- **Effective Interest Rate**: Actual interest rate considering prepayments
- **Total Months**: Actual loan duration with prepayments

### **Export Functionality**
- Click **"Export CSV"** to download your repayment schedule
- Open in Excel or Google Sheets for further analysis
- Includes all columns with proper formatting

### **Reset Feature**
- Click **"Reset"** to clear all data and start fresh
- Useful when comparing different loan scenarios

## 🔍 Understanding the Repayment Schedule

### **Table Columns Explained**
1. **Month**: Sequential month number
2. **Date**: Actual payment date
3. **Opening Balance**: Loan balance at start of month
4. **Principal**: Amount going toward loan principal
5. **Interest**: Interest charged for the month
6. **EMI**: Total monthly payment (Principal + Interest)
7. **Prepayment**: Extra amount you pay (editable)
8. **Closing Balance**: Remaining balance after the month

### **Key Insights from the Schedule**
- **Early Years**: Most payment goes to interest
- **Later Years**: Most payment goes to principal
- **Prepayment Impact**: Reduces both time and interest
- **Balance Progression**: Shows how your debt decreases
